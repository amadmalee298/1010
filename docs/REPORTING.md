# Report definitions

All reports take an inclusive Bangkok date range (max 366 days) and exclude cancelled orders.

| Figure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Gross sales | Σ order subtotal (list price × quantity) |
| Discounts | Σ promotion + manual + points discounts |
| Net sales | Σ (order total − VAT) − refunds ex-VAT |
| COGS | Σ order COGS (weighted average cost at sale) − COGS returned to stock by refunds |
| Gross profit | Net sales − COGS |
| Gross margin | Gross profit ÷ net sales |
| Waste | Cost of WASTE movements in the period |
| Shrinkage | Cost of negative stock-count adjustments in the period |
| Operating expenses | Non-voided expenses by category (purchases are excluded — they are inventory) |
| Net profit | Gross profit − waste − shrinkage − operating expenses |
| Product net sales | Line total × (order total − VAT) ÷ subtotal, net of refunded quantity (discounts allocated pro-rata) |
| Menu profitability | Product report sorted by gross profit, with margin and share of sales |
| Expected cash | Σ drawer ledger for the session; variance = counted − expected |
